MCW

Text-aware video compression for screen recordings

Neural text detection (CRAFT + RefineNet) drives adaptive DCT-quadtree partitioning: text regions get high-quality encoding, backgrounds get aggressive compression, and SSIM-based temporal filtering skips redundant frames. CPU-deployable at 1080p/30fps.
